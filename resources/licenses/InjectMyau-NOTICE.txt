The KillAura and Scaffold algorithms and their movement/rotation utilities are
located in src/moon/port/myau/. Original package names were relocated to
moon.port.myau. LWJGL input imports use the destination org.lwjglx bridge.
Moon-specific lifecycle, settings, config, event and rendering adaptations are
in src/moon/client/McpMyauModules.java and the Minecraft integration hooks.

No injector, DLL, native bootstrap or Forge runtime from the archive is used.
Optional upstream modules outside the requested KillAura/Scaffold scope are
represented as disabled/uninstalled in MyauPeers.java.
